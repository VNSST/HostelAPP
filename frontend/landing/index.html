How It Works

Get started in three simple steps. No complex setup required.

1

Choose Your Role

Open the app and select whether you're a Hostel Owner or a Tenant. Each role gets a tailored experience.

2

Verify & Register

Verify your phone number with OTP, fill in your details, and you're in. Owners create a hostel ID; tenants join using it.

3

Manage Payments

Tenants submit payment proofs. Owners verify via dashboard, reconcile UPI statements, and approve cash payments.
